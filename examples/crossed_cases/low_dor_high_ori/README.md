# Crossed Cases: Low DoR + High ORI

**Criterion**: DoR < Q1 AND ORI > Q3

Cases where models provide **shallow reasoning** yet produce **creative, original responses**. This combination suggests surface-level engagement that nonetheless yields unconventional or novel framings, the model may skip analytical depth but arrive at distinctive formulations.

## Characteristics

- Brief or superficial justifications
- Limited step-by-step reasoning
- Unexpected or creative phrasings
- Novel approaches despite minimal elaboration

## Selection

**10 examples** drawn from the N=500 sample.
