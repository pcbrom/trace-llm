# Crossed Cases: High DoR + Low ORI

**Criterion**: DoR > Q3 AND ORI < Q1

Cases where models demonstrate **deep, thorough reasoning** yet produce **conventional, unoriginal responses**. This combination suggests that exhaustive analysis does not necessarily lead to creative or novel approaches, the model may reason deeply but remain within predictable patterns.

## Characteristics

- Extensive step-by-step reasoning chains
- Comprehensive problem decomposition
- Formulaic or template-based conclusions
- Conventional problem-solving despite thorough analysis

## Selection

**10 examples** drawn from the N=500 sample.
